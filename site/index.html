The session

Expected contributions

Research on precipitation, evapotranspiration or both, across scales, climates and hydrological contexts.

01

Evaluation, benchmarking & uncertainty

Compare products across hydroclimates and scales; assess biases, uncertainty and water-balance consistency.

02

Dataset development, fusion & integration

Advance estimation and combine observations, remote sensing, reanalysis and model-based information.

03

Hydrological modelling & applications

Test how dataset choice affects model calibration, prediction, drought monitoring and flood assessments.

04

Emerging datasets & future directions

Explore new satellite missions, high-resolution products, evaluation standards and shared workflows.
