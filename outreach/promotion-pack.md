## LinkedIn post

Which precipitation and evapotranspiration datasets are right for your hydrological study?

At #EGU27, our session HS3.8 brings together researchers developing, evaluating and applying these datasets:

Precipitation and Evapotranspiration Datasets for Hydrological Studies: Development, Evaluation, and Applications

We invite contributions on:
• Comparing products across regions and scales
• Understanding biases, uncertainty and water-balance consistency
• Developing and combining datasets
• Testing how dataset choice affects hydrological modelling and assessments

If you develop precipitation or evapotranspiration datasets—or use them in your research—we would love to hear from you.

## Newsletter announcement

**Headline:** Call for abstracts: precipitation and evapotranspiration datasets at EGU27

We invite submissions to EGU27 session **HS3.8: Precipitation and Evapotranspiration Datasets for Hydrological Studies: Development, Evaluation, and Applications**.

The session welcomes work on dataset development, evaluation and hydrological applications, including benchmarking, uncertainty, data integration and water-balance consistency. Join the discussion in Vienna or online, **4–9 April 2027**.

**Abstract deadline: 12 January 2027, 13:00 CET.**

**Travel support deadline: 1 December 2026, 13:00 CET.** Eligible Roland Schlich applicants must submit their abstract and support request by this earlier deadline. [Check eligibility and apply](https://www.egu27.eu/attendance/financial_support_and_waivers.html).
